Delete your account

Onmenu: Family Recipe Book

In the app

  1. Open Onmenu and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email hello@onmenu.recipes from the address you sign in with (your Apple or Google email), asking us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What's deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Recipe books that only you are in, and the recipes in them.
  • Your notes and reviews on any recipe.
  • Your place in books shared with you, and any invites you've made.
  • Your settings and import history.

What's kept

  • Recipes you added to a book shared with other people stay in that book for them, shown as by "Deleted user". Your name and email aren't kept with them. If you'd rather remove them, delete those recipes before deleting your account.
  • If you bought Onmenu Pro, the purchase record is kept by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat (who handles purchases for us), as their policies and the law require.
